How To Play

  • MoveUse the left joystick to change lanes and move freely up and down the road.
  • AttackTap the right sword button to slash. If you miss, you will only hear the blade cut through the air.
  • DashUse the dash button above attack to evade danger quickly. Dash gives you a very short escape window.
  • ComboChain three hits to trigger a finisher. The last strike is stronger and can reward power-ups.
  • SurviveDodge traffic, read enemy attack warnings, and use your stored power-up at the right moment.
